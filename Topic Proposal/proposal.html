FitHea: A Unified Fitness and Diet Tracking System

Problem/Data Domain

Problem/Data Domain

In recent years, maintaining a healthy lifestyle through fitness and diet has become an increasingly important goal for many people. However, these two facets of the health domain are often viewed as independent realms rather than as interconnected components within a unified system. Consequently, users frequently struggle to determine whether their dietary choices effectively support their three primary fitness objectives: fat loss, muscle gain, or the maintenance of overall health.

The core information problem lies in the fragmentation of health-related data and the lack of integration between different types of user-generated information. Existing fitness and diet applications, such as myfitnesspal or FITBOD, They often focus on a specific aspect of calorie tracking or exercise planning, yet rarely offer an integrated system that seamlessly connects the two. This fragmentation makes it difficult for users to gain a holistic understanding of their data, leading to confusion, inconsistent tracking habits, and ultimately undermining the long-term engagement of users motivated to improve their fitness.

Therefore, we believe there is an urgent need for an interactive system capable of integrating fitness and dietary data, in order to help users better understand the correlation between their behaviors and health outcomes. A more integrated and user-centric solution would help enhance the system's usability and improve the effectiveness of long-term health management.
